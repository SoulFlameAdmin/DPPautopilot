# DPP STARTER DOCUMENTS v0.1

Status: **DEMO / APPROVAL ONLY**

Live approval surface: `/starter-documents`

This package introduces four customer-facing demo concepts without changing production DPP data or production business logic:

1. QR Carrier Demo — physical QR/data-carrier concept.
2. Battery Passport Demo — visual preview of a public Digital Product Passport.
3. Invoice + DPP QR Demo — optional SoulFlame commercial integration; not a mandatory Battery Passport artifact.
4. DPP Autopilot Dashboard Demo — illustrative manufacturer/product UI.

## Safety / compliance boundary

- Demo values are synthetic unless explicitly loaded from the live public API.
- Do not interpret a demo design, ACTIVE badge or invoice as legal certification.
- The production Digital Product Passport remains the electronic record exposed through the public passport route and related controlled-access services.
- Manufacturer, carbon-footprint, recycled-content, warranty and other compliance-sensitive data must come from verified source data before production display.

After Mitko approves v0.1, the visual direction may be promoted into production templates. The next planned approval set is v0.2: Create Passport, Public Passport, Compliance & Data Access, and CSV/API Import.
