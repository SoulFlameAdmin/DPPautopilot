# DAVID DPP — Ilko self-service customer onboarding (MVP)

**Build status:** PR #353 only, stacked on PR #350. **Not production deployed or commercially accepted.**

## Customer journey — zero technical jargon

1. Ilko receives an approved DPP campaign link from Admin. Planned canonical path: `https://dpp-autopilot.vercel.app/start?ref=ilko` **AFTER** production release only. The `ref` is a non-authoritative campaign hint; it must not count as an earned commission.
2. Ilko sends the link to a business contact after a consensual sales conversation. Do not send any login credentials or personal customer tokens.
3. Customer clicks **Започнете безплатно** and uses existing `/company` verified-email authentication. For same-browser signup/login the page resumes `/setup?ref=ilko` automatically after the session is confirmed. Otherwise customer uses the **Продължи с DAVID** link in their account.
4. If the business does not yet have a tenant, enter the firm name once; the existing API creates the company and caller-owner membership atomically.
5. DAVID asks eight short questions about company, battery category, models, serial/batch, data/evidence, scale, import and QR workflow. Gemini adapts phrasing when `GEMINI_API_KEY` is configured and the customer explicitly permits sending their answers to Gemini. Otherwise truthful guided template questions work without Google.
6. Customer reviews/edits the eight answers and explicitly approves. API validates an authenticated tenant owner/admin, writes exactly eight bounded answers through the existing organization-scoped DB RPC, then invokes the **existing** `dpp_api_manufacturer_onboarding_configure()` function. No dynamic code, SQL, arbitrary roles or database schema are generated by Gemini.
7. Workspace now displays the firm configuration with a revision number. Customer may create a battery model, item and **draft passport** via existing protected model/item/passport APIs by supplying their **real model ID and unique serial**.
8. Missing technical, supplier, carbon, recycling and other mandatory evidence must be added by the manufacturer. Once a real passport passes the existing sector-specific activation gate, `/api/qr?identifier=...` creates a QR pointing at its **active public passport**; draft QR is deliberately unavailable.

## Operator / admin responsibilities

- **Ilko:** sales contact and explanation. He does not need DPP Admin or the manufacturer's private data. The URL `ref=ilko` is **not** proof of referral. CRM should attribute only verified lead ID + assigned operator + consent + company approval.
- **Owner / Borko:** review sales/commission records separately from manufacturers' tenant data. Do not share a service-role key with DAVID, Gemini or operators.
- **Manufacturer:** owns final data, approves personalization and legally relevant claims. Drafting does not equal legal compliance.
- **DAVID / Gemini:** asks questions and proposes safe configuration; cannot autonomously submit EU Registry, activate incomplete passports, publish a legal certificate or charge a customer.

## Existing production DB reused — no schema writes in this PR

- `dpp_api_manufacturer_onboarding_get()`
- `dpp_api_manufacturer_onboarding_answer_upsert(text,text,jsonb)`
- `dpp_api_manufacturer_onboarding_configure()`
- `dpp_api_tenant_context()`
- `dpp_api_organization_create(text,text)`
- `dpp_api_models_create`, `dpp_api_items_create`, `dpp_api_passport_create`
- `dpp_api_passport_public_resolve` and QR rendering only for `active`.

The eight answer writes are parallel/idempotent per question key, followed by the configuration RPC only if **all eight** succeed. A failure can leave a partially completed answer set; retry is safe through upserts. Data is scoped by the authenticated active tenant, not by the client-supplied company ID.

## Privacy and Google integration

- Add `GEMINI_API_KEY` as a **server-only** Vercel environment variable. Optional `GEMINI_MODEL` selects an authorized model; default currently `gemini-3.8-flash`, subject to provider availability.
- Gemini uses Google API's documented `generateContent` with response JSON schema. Key is sent only in `x-goog-api-key` from the server; no client-side key and no user-controlled provider URL.
- Obtain suitable consent/disclosure and GDPR processor/DPA/data-transfer evaluation for customer-supplied business data BEFORE enabling Gemini. Customer can leave opt-in unchecked and still finish DPP setup.
- Rate limits: existing local and opt-in shared distributed protections; cost caps and operational abuse controls require production measurement.
- No real Gmail delivery, AI billing or mobile/browser E2E has been proven by tests in this PR.
