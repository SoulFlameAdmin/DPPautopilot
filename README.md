# DPP Autopilot

Battery Digital Product Passport automation platform.

## Current state

Project initialized on 2026-09-17. The product dashboard reads the master plan from `data/master-plan.json` and DAVID worker state from `data/worker-status.json`.

## Product gates

1. DEMO — show the concept with realistic battery data and a QR-accessible passport.
2. MVP — usable by a pilot customer with validation, roles, audit trail and registry test workflow.
3. PROPOSAL READY — sales/demo package, onboarding flow and pilot contract scope are clear.
4. PRODUCTION — secure, tested and operational for real customers.
5. EXPANDED — multi-tenant, ERP/BMS connectors, automation and additional DPP sectors.

## Regulatory anchors

- EU Battery Regulation (EU) 2023/1542, Article 77 and Annex XIII.
- Battery passport requirement starts 18 February 2027 for LMT batteries, industrial batteries >2 kWh and EV batteries placed on the EU market or put into service.
- EU DPP Registry became operational on 20 July 2026 and provides a testing environment.

This repository is an engineering project, not legal advice. Compliance claims must be verified against the current EU rules before production use.
